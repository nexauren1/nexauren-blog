Blur & Pixelate FAQ

Can I blur only one area?

Yes. Select “Selected area”, then drag a rectangle over the part of the image that should be affected.

Does the tool upload my photo?

No. Image rendering and export happen locally in the browser.

Is blur a guarantee of privacy?

No visual redaction should be treated as infallible. Check the exported image at full resolution before sharing sensitive material.
