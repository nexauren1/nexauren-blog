SEARCH GUIDE

Rotate or mirror an image in your browser

Use this tool for sideways photos, mirrored graphics, scanned pages, product shots, and quick image corrections. The transformation is rendered locally in the browser.

You can rotate by -90°, +90°, or 180°, apply horizontal or vertical flipping, and choose JPG, PNG, or WebP output.

Use caseFix sideways phone photos
WorkflowMirror product or design images
Local processingWork in your browser without sending the source image to a processing server.

Common ways to use this tool

  • Fix sideways phone photos
  • Mirror product or design images
  • Rotate scanned documents before sharing
  • Prepare images for another editing workflow

Nexauren Rotate & Flip Image FAQ

Can I rotate an image by 90 degrees?

Yes. The tool provides 90-degree rotation controls plus 180 degrees and reset.

Can I flip an image horizontally?

Yes. You can mirror images horizontally, vertically, or both.

Is processing local?

Yes. The transformation is processed directly on the device in the browser.
