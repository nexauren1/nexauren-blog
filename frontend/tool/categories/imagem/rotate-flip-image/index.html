Rotate & Flip FAQ

Does rotation change image dimensions?

Yes. A 90° or 270° rotation swaps width and height in the exported canvas.

Can I combine rotation and flipping?

Yes. Rotate and flip controls can be combined before export.

Is processing local?

Yes. The source image is processed in your browser.
