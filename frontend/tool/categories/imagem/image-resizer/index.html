Resize an image to exact pixel dimensions

Nexauren Image Resizer lets you change an image to a precise width and height without installing desktop software. Enter the target dimensions, keep the aspect ratio when you want proportional scaling, or use a fit mode when you need a fixed canvas.

It is useful for website graphics, profile pictures, product images, thumbnails, blog images, email attachments, Open Graph previews, and social media assets.

1. Select your imagesAdd one image or a batch from your phone or computer. Camera capture remains a separate option on mobile.
2. Choose the dimensionsEnter the exact pixel width and height, lock the aspect ratio, or start with a preset size.
3. Preview and downloadCheck original and resized previews, then download each result or the full batch.

Common image resize use cases

Resize images for websites

Use dimensions that match the space where an image will be displayed. Smaller display sizes can reduce unnecessary pixel data.

Resize images for social media

Use the square, portrait, landscape, or Open Graph presets as starting points, then fine-tune the dimensions for your design.

Resize images to a required size

Forms, marketplaces, templates, and publishing workflows can require exact pixel dimensions. Enter the required width and height instead of estimating.

Image Resizer FAQ

Is Nexauren Image Resizer free?

Yes. The tool is available in the browser without a paid batch quota. Your browser performs the resizing locally.

Can I resize an image to 1080 × 1080?

Yes. Choose the 1080 × 1080 preset or enter 1080 for both width and height.

Can I keep the original aspect ratio?

Yes. Keep the aspect-ratio option enabled while editing width or height.

Can I resize multiple images at once?

Yes. Add multiple images to the queue and run the resize operation as a batch.

Does resizing upload my images?

No. Resizing is performed locally in your browser. Your device and browser still determine how much data can be processed comfortably.

Which formats can I export?

You can export results as WebP, JPG, or PNG. JPG and WebP use the quality control; PNG export is lossless.
