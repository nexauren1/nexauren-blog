Image Converter FAQ

Is Nexauren Image Converter free?

Yes. The browser tool is available without a paid conversion quota. Practical limits come from the capabilities and memory available in the browser and device.

Does the converter upload my images?

No. Image conversion is performed locally in your browser, so a source image does not need to be uploaded to Nexauren for the conversion itself.

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes. Add several supported image files to the queue and convert them as one batch.

Can I resize while converting?

Yes. Switch Resize to “Fit within maximum size”, then enter the maximum width and height. The aspect-ratio option preserves proportions.

Can I convert PNG to JPG?

Yes. JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are rendered onto the selected background.

What happens if AVIF is not supported?

The browser may not provide an AVIF encoder. Nexauren detects that case during export and can fall back to WebP while clearly marking the result.
