Social Image Resizer FAQ

Are the canvas dimensions exact?

Yes. The generated output uses the selected width and height.

What is the difference between Cover and Contain?

Cover fills the canvas and may crop part of the source. Contain keeps the full source visible and uses the selected background around it.

Does the safe-zone guide appear in the download?

No. The safe-zone guide is a preview-only overlay and is never drawn into the exported image.

Can I add text?

Yes. Add optional text, choose its color, position, and size, then generate the final image.

Is processing local?

Yes. The image is rendered in your browser and does not need to be uploaded for processing.
