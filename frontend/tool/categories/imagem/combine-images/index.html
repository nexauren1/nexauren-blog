Image Combiner FAQ

Does the download keep full resolution?

Yes. The preview is scaled for the interface, while the generated file is rendered again at the calculated output dimensions, subject to device memory limits.

Can I change image order?

Yes. Each queued image has move-up, move-down, and remove controls.

Can images have different dimensions?

Yes. Each source keeps its original pixel dimensions in the composition.

Is processing local?

Yes. Images are loaded and rendered in your browser on your device.
