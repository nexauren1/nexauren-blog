Image Cropper FAQ

Is the image cropper free?

Yes. The cropper runs in your browser without a paid crop quota.

Can I drag the crop position?

Yes. Drag inside the image to move the crop horizontally and vertically, or use the position sliders for precise control.

Which aspect ratios are available?

The cropper includes 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 9:16, 3:2, 4:5, a full-image option, and a custom ratio.

Does cropping upload my image?

No. The crop is processed locally in the browser on your device.

Can I export JPG, PNG, and WebP?

Yes. Choose the output format and quality before generating the crop.
