Blur or pixelate a specific part of an image

This tool is useful when you need to make a detail less recognizable in a screenshot, photo, profile image, document preview, or other visual.

Select an effect and intensity, then choose whether to process the whole image or a selected region. The image stays on your device during processing.

Nexauren Blur & Pixelate FAQ

Is the image uploaded?

The image processing happens in the browser on your device.

Can I blur only part of an image?

Yes. Use the area selection mode and drag over the region you want to process.

Can I pixelate instead of blur?

Yes. The tool provides both blur and pixelation effects.
