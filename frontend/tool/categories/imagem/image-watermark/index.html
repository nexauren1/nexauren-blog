Watermark Studio FAQ

Does the watermark tool upload my image?

No. The image is rendered and exported locally in your browser.

Can I repeat the watermark?

Yes. Enable the repeat option to create a tiled watermark pattern.

Can I control the watermark position?

Yes. Choose a corner or center position and adjust styling before exporting.

Which output formats are available?

WebP, JPG, and PNG are available. PNG is exported without lossy quality compression.
