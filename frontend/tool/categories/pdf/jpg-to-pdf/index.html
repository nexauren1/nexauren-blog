FAQ

Can I combine multiple JPG images into one PDF?

Yes. Each image becomes a PDF page in the selected order.

Can I reorder pages?

Yes. Use the up and down controls in the image queue.

Does the tool upload my JPG files?

No server upload is required for the conversion workflow.

Can I use the camera on mobile?

Yes. The camera control uses the device camera input and adds the captured image to the queue.
