Insert pages from one PDF into another

Combine pages from a secondary PDF with a main document without opening a desktop editor. Choose the source PDF, the pages to insert, and whether they should appear before, after, or at the end of a reference page.

This is useful for adding appendices, replacing sections, combining forms, or building a final document from pages stored in separate PDF files.

  • Insert pages after a selected page
  • Append pages to the end of a PDF
  • Keep both source PDFs on the device

Nexauren PDF Page Inserter FAQ

Can I insert pages from another PDF?

Yes. Select a main PDF and a second PDF containing the pages you want to copy.

Can I choose where the pages are inserted?

Yes. The tool provides before, after, and end-of-document options.

Is the original PDF changed?

No. The tool creates a new exported PDF.
